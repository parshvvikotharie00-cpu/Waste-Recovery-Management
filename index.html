RESEARCH & METHODOLOGY

Knowledge Hub

Interpret waste indicators and understand the platform's decision-support approach.

Waste generation vs recovery

Generated waste describes the estimated quantity arising in a reporting period. Utilized, processed or recovered quantities represent material directed to beneficial use or processing. The remaining gap indicates potential recovery needs.

CRROM and opportunity indices

Composite opportunity scores are relative prioritization aids calculated in the supplied research dataset. They are not independently verified government rankings or a direct percentage of waste recovered.

GIS interpretation

Hotspots indicate comparative regional opportunity. State-centre map markers are illustrative and should not be interpreted as exact facility coordinates.

Data quality and limitations

Figures are based on the supplied project dataset and its source periods. Data availability and reporting conventions vary by sector. Contact records require independent verification before commercial decisions.
