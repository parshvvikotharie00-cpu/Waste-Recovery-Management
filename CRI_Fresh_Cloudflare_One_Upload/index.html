KNOWLEDGE HUB

Understanding circular recovery

How waste intelligence informs circular resource decisions.

🌾 Agricultural biomass

Crop residues can support composting, bioenergy and industrial feedstocks. Regional surplus is an opportunity indicator, not a guaranteed recoverable quantity.

♻️ Plastic waste

Segregation, collection and material-specific processing influence recycling outcomes. Generation estimates alone do not establish recycling capacity.

🔌 Electronic waste

Formal collection, safe dismantling and authorized processing are important for material recovery and pollution prevention.

How to interpret the opportunity index

Scores are project-derived relative indicators intended to support prioritization. They are not direct measurements of available buyers, processing facilities or guaranteed revenue.
